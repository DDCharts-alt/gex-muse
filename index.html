GEX Heatmap

Dealer gamma exposure by strike × expiry — plain-English positioning map
Strike
Call wall (positive GEX) Put wall (negative GEX) Current price
Wall
Strike with outsized gamma — magnets price toward it.
Flip (zero-gamma)
The level where dealer hedging switches direction. Above it = stable; a cross = regime change.
Max pain
Strike where option holders lose the most at expiry — price drifts toward it into expiry.
Expected move
What the at-the-money straddle is pricing for the move by expiry.
Glue / Bleed
Glue = positive-GEX chop (price sticks). Bleed = slow drift toward a magnet.
